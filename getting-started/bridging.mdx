Your Aura balance is shown in **USD**. Under the hood trading settles in
stablecoins on Alephium, but you never need to think about chains, bridges,
or gas. Deposits are credited to your USD balance, and withdrawals arrive as
stablecoins wherever you want them.

## Depositing

Open **Deposit** in the top navigation and pick the path that matches where
your money is:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="From another chain" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Send **USDC or USDT** on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, or BNB Chain to your
    personal deposit address. Most deposits are credited to your USD balance
    within moments of confirming on the source chain.
  </Card>
  <Card title="With a card" icon="credit-card">
    Buy stablecoins with a debit or credit card through the built-in onramp.
    They arrive at your deposit address and are credited the same way.
  </Card>
  <Card title="On Alephium" icon="bolt">
    Already on Alephium? Send USDT directly to your wallet address for a
    near-instant credit with no extra steps.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Need help?" icon="life-ring">
    The deposit dialog shows the exact tokens and chains supported right now,
    plus live status for each deposit.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the deposit dialog">
    Click **Deposit** and choose your source chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send to your personal address">
    Copy your deposit address (or scan the QR) and send USDC/USDT from any
    wallet or exchange.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get credited in USD">
    Your balance updates automatically once the deposit confirms on the source
    chain, usually within a couple of minutes. Larger amounts take the routed
    path and land in 30 to 90 minutes, and the dialog tracks the status of
    every deposit either way.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Deposit addresses are **yours** and stay valid, so you can send to the same
  address again next time or withdraw from an exchange straight to it.
</Note>

## How big amounts settle

There is no maximum. Anything you send gets credited, and the size only
decides how fast and at what rate:

| Tier | Applies to | Arrives in | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Instant** | Amounts inside the live limits shown in the dialog | 1 to 3 minutes | 0.30% |
| **Routed** | Anything larger | 30 to 90 minutes | 0.10% plus the actual network cost of moving the funds |

Instant deposits are paid out of stablecoin inventory we hold on Alephium, so
they land as soon as your source chain confirms. Larger amounts take the routed
path, where your funds travel the canonical rails themselves and get credited
on arrival. That path uses none of our inventory, which is why its percentage
is lower. On a large transfer it usually works out cheaper overall than the
instant rate.

You never pick a tier. The dialog tells you which one applies, the exact fee,
and the ETA before you send anything.

## Fees

| Charge | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Deposit fee | 0.30% instant, or 0.10% plus pass-through costs when routed |
| Withdrawal fee | Same as deposits |
| Source-chain gas | Whatever the chain you send from charges, or the card provider's own fee |

Withdrawal quotes show the exact amount that will arrive after fees, and you
see it before you confirm.

## Withdrawing

Open **Withdraw** and choose where you want the funds:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a destination">
    A supported EVM chain (stablecoins to any address you control) or a
    direct Alephium transfer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the quote">
    You see the fee and the exact amount that will arrive before you commit to
    anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it's really you">
    Because the destination sits outside Aura, withdrawals ask for a fresh
    confirmation with your passkey or an email code. The destination, chain,
    and amount are shown on that screen, and the approval covers only those
    exact details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get paid">
    Withdrawals settle on the same two tiers as deposits. Most land within a
    couple of minutes, and larger ones take the routed path and arrive in 30 to
    90 minutes. The quote tells you which applies, and the dialog tracks the
    withdrawal until it lands.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Withdrawing **USDT** to a chain that has no routed lane for it can be
  declined with a message suggesting USDC instead. Picking USDC for that
  destination goes through normally.
</Note>

## What about gas?

You don't hold ALPH (Alephium's gas token) and you never see gas prompts.
When an action needs network fees, Aura converts a few cents of your USD
balance at a posted rate and shows it as a small "network fees" line, always
denominated in USD. Your first deposit includes a small starting amount of
network credit, and its cost comes out of the credited balance. See
[Alephium (ALPH)](/tokens/alph) for details.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Deposit not credited yet?">
    - Check the deposit dialog, which shows live status for each deposit.
    - Confirm the transaction succeeded on the source chain (block explorer).
    - Large deposits take the routed path, so allow 30 to 90 minutes. The
      dialog shows which tier your deposit is on.
    - Deposits below the minimum are paused for review rather than credited.
      Contact support and we'll sort it out.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sent an unsupported token or chain?">
    Only send the tokens and chains listed in the deposit dialog. If you sent
    something else, contact support on
    [Telegram](https://t.me/AuraMarkets) with the transaction hash before
    doing anything else.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Withdrawal pending?">
    Most withdrawals settle within a couple of minutes. Routed ones take 30 to
    90 minutes and show progress updates as they go. If a withdrawal looks
    stuck beyond that, contact support with its ID from the dialog.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
