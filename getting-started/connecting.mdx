Aura has a built-in wallet, so getting started feels like signing up for any
modern app. There are no browser extensions to install and no key material to
manage. Your balance is shown in **USD**, and you can fund it from major
chains or with a card.

## Creating your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Aura">
    Visit [aura.markets](https://aura.markets/) and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign up with email or Google">
    Enter your email and type the one-time code we send you, or continue with
    Google. Starting here means your account is recoverable from the moment it
    exists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a passkey (optional)">
    Turn on Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, or your device PIN for one-tap
    sign-in next time. You can skip this and add it later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    Your wallet is ready with a generated username you can change any time in
    **Settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your email or Google login is how you get back in from any device, so there is
nothing you have to write down and store. See
[Your Aura Wallet](/getting-started/passkeys) for how the wallet works
underneath, where your key lives, and how to enable recovery-phrase export if
you want it.

<Tip>
  Once connected, head to [Deposits & Withdrawals](/getting-started/bridging)
  to fund your account from Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, a card, or
  directly on Alephium.
</Tip>
