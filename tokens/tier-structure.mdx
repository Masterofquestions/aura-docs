Aura utilizes a tiered staking (vaulting) system powered by our native \$AURA
token. Each tier grants unique privileges, increasing your ability to
influence governance decisions, propose new markets, and earn higher fee
shares.

## Tiers at a glance

| Tier | AURA required | Proposal votes | Creator fee points | Share of the 250-point trading fee |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Degenerate | 1,000,000 | 30 | 100 | 40% |
| Diamond | 833,333 | 22 | 83 | 33.2% |
| Platinum | 666,667 | 16 | 67 | 26.8% |
| Gold | 500,000 | 11 | 50 | 20% |
| Silver | 333,333 | 7 | 33 | 13.2% |
| Bronze | 166,667 | 3 | 17 | 6.8% |
| Iron | 60,000 | 1 | 6 | 2.4% |
| Builder | 25,000 | 0 | 3 | 1.2% |
| Scout | 10,000 | 0 | 1 | 0.4% |

Below Scout, personal proposal capacity and creator points are zero. Scout and Builder can create proposals but cannot vote on proposals or join Oracle rounds. Proposal-vote weights are distinct from Oracle participation, which has its own stake activation and round rules.

Personal creation capacity is the whole number of 10,000-AURA units in active stake, with uses retained for a rolling 24 hours. It is not capped at a tier's minimum-stake example. Linked-bundle size has its own tier limit.

## Fee sharing explained

The total trading fee is 250 points, or 2.5% of executed volume. Creator points are carved from it: Scout starts at one point (0.01% of volume, or 0.4% of the fee), and Degenerate at 100 points (1% of volume, or 40% of the fee). Token amounts use integer arithmetic. These are creator allocations before any accepted sharing arrangements.

The rate starts with the slot owner's tier at counted proposal use. Falling below a tier threshold reduces the rate on dependent markets permanently; restaking cannot restore an older market's rate. New stake can support a later proposal. For borrowed slots, the slot owner supplies this tier history, while the public creator and recorded reward recipients keep their separate roles.

See [Creator rewards](/programs/creator-rewards) for automatic distribution and recorded sharing.
