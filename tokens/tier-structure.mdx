Aura utilizes a tiered staking (vaulting) system powered by our native $AURA
token. Each tier grants unique privileges, increasing your ability to
influence governance decisions, propose new markets, and earn higher fee
shares.

## Tiers at a glance

| Tier | Voting Power | Creator's share of the 2.5% trading fee | Equivalent on volume | Stake Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Degenerate** | 30 votes | 30% | 0.75% | 1,000,000 $AURA |
| **Diamond** | 22 votes | 22% | 0.55% | 833,333 $AURA |
| **Platinum** | 16 votes | 16% | 0.40% | 666,667 $AURA |
| **Gold** | 11 votes | 11.2% | 0.28% | 500,000 $AURA |
| **Silver** | 7 votes | 6.8% | 0.17% | 333,333 $AURA |
| **Bronze** | 3 votes | 2.8% | 0.07% | 166,667 $AURA |
| **Iron** | 1 vote | 0.8% | 0.02% | 60,000 $AURA |
| None | 0 votes | 0% | 0% | &lt;60,000 $AURA |

The fee column is a share of the **2.5% trading fee**, not of the traded
amount. The last column restates it against volume so the two can't be
confused.

## Fee sharing explained

Each market created on Aura generates fees from user transactions. Your tier
directly determines your share of these fees:

- Higher tiers receive a larger percentage of market-generated fees.
- Lower tiers receive smaller shares but still earn passive rewards for their
  contributions.

For example, a **Degenerate**-tier creator takes 30% of the trading fee their
markets generate, which is 0.75% of the volume traded on them. A **Bronze**-tier
creator takes a smaller 2.8% of the fee, or 0.07% of volume.

Your cut on each fill is the **lower** of your tier when you proposed the market
and your tier at that moment. So letting your stake fall (or requesting an
unlock, which drops your tier immediately) reduces what you earn on markets you
already created, while climbing higher later does not raise it above the tier you
proposed at. Stake before you propose.

This incentivizes community members to stake higher amounts of $AURA,
promoting active governance participation and quality market creation.
