The **Aura Vault** is the on-chain home of every staked \$AURA token. It
does three jobs at once:

1. **Tracks tier eligibility.** The tier you qualify for (and the
   benefits that come with it) is computed live from your vaulted
   balance.
2. **Powers governance voting.** Your voting weight in proposal voting
   and dispute resolution is read directly from the Vault.
3. **Backs the optimistic oracle with skin in the game.** When a
   dispute is resolved, voters who backed the wrong outcome (or who
   committed a vote and never revealed it) have a portion of their
   vaulted \$AURA **slashed**. That \$AURA flows to the voters who got
   the call right.

## How to stake tokens

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Sign in to your Aura account and open its built-in wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Vault">
    Click the **Vault** tab in the navigation toolbar at the top of the
    site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your tier">
    Enter the number of \$AURA tokens you wish to vault. Your tier is
    assigned automatically based on the amount staked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your stake">
    Confirm the staking transaction in your wallet. Your new tier
    status takes effect upon confirmation. Oracle participation activates separately after its 24-hour delay and keeper processing; creator eligibility does not prove Oracle eligibility.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to unlock tokens

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Vault">
    Sign in and open the Vault page. It shows your active stake, any pending request and the currently available unlock amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request unlock">
    Choose an amount within the displayed limit and confirm. Only one request may be outstanding. Its seven-day waiting period starts, and the requested amount immediately stops contributing to your creator tier and capacity. Recent proposal uses and outgoing slot allocations can limit the amount available to request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow automatic delivery">
    After seven days, Aura automatically delivers the free portion to the original wallet. There is no manual Withdraw tab or claim transaction. Exact funded penalties captured before the exit cutoff can retain part of the request. Released funds become eligible under the original maturity; a new dispute cannot restart the wait.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Penalty exposure is bounded when it is admitted, both for each owner and across the Vault. Only exact funded claims captured before an exit cutoff can retain that exit's funds; each claim is collected or released once. Those already funded claims are not clamped to 1% of the Vault's later, smaller balance. Ordinary unreserved slashing retains its separate circuit breaker. New disputes cannot keep adding obligations to an existing exit indefinitely.

Delivery still depends on working services and confirmed chain execution. A submitted payment or an unavailable balance read does not prove completion. The Vault page lets you retry a failed read and follows a remaining request until it is delivered or fully accounted for.

## Slashing: why staking has skin in the game

A pure governance system that only rewards good behavior, without
penalizing bad behavior, is easy to game. A whale who votes the same
way on every dispute earns rewards on the ones they get right and pays
nothing on the ones they get wrong. That breaks the alignment Aura
needs: voters should only weigh in on outcomes they actually believe.

Slashing fixes the asymmetry. By making wrong-side votes (and silent
commits) cost real \$AURA, the cost of voting on a market you don't
understand is roughly equal to the reward of voting on one you do. The
result is an honest oracle where the most confident, best-informed
voters get most of the weight.

### How slashing works

Slashing follows the dispute lifecycle and needs nothing from you. The penalty
transactions themselves are permissionless, and Aura's automation submits them
as each phase closes:

| Voter behavior | What happens | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revealed a vote for the **winning** outcome | Earns a proportional share of the penalty pool. | After the vote finalizes |
| Revealed a vote for a **losing** outcome | **0.1% of vaulted \$AURA** is slashed. | After the vote finalizes |
| Committed a vote but **never revealed** | **0.1% of vaulted \$AURA** is slashed. | After the vote finalizes |
| Was eligible (Iron tier+) but **didn't vote at all** | **0.1% of vaulted \$AURA** is slashed. | During the reveal window |

Every slashed token flows into the **penalty pool** for that specific
market. Once a YES/NO pool is finalized, Aura automatically distributes the
recorded winning-voter allocations as \$AURA. There is no user claim action.
A TOO_EARLY round retains its pool for a later round.

<Tip>
  The slash percentage is small per individual vote (0.1%), but it
  compounds over time for stakers who consistently vote against the
  community consensus. Long-term, the only sustainable strategy is to
  vote on disputes you've actually researched.
</Tip>

### Stake at risk and exit protection

Round eligibility and penalty backing are captured under the Oracle's activation and round rules. A later balance read does not redefine an already funded claim. Penalties do not reach your USDT or trading positions, or another user's stake.

Penalty exposure is bounded when it is admitted, both for each owner and across the Vault. Only exact funded claims captured before an exit cutoff can retain that exit's funds; each claim is collected or released once. Those already funded claims are not clamped to 1% of the Vault's later, smaller balance. Ordinary unreserved slashing retains its separate circuit breaker. New disputes cannot keep adding obligations to an existing exit indefinitely.

### Authority over slashing

Only the **Optimistic Oracle contract** can call the Vault's slash
function, and even then only as part of a dispute resolution flow
that follows the recorded round and phase rules. Some non-voter processing
occurs during reveal; the dedicated penalty passes must complete before the
reward pool is finalized.

This authority is rotated through a **timelocked pointer**: changing
which contract can slash requires queuing a new slasher address and
waiting through a delay window before the change takes effect. There
is no way to push an emergency slash through with no notice.

Finally, staking is restricted to **regular wallet addresses** (EOAs
and multisigs). Smart contracts cannot stake, because several payout and
refund paths must create outputs directly to the staker, which is
impossible for contract addresses and could otherwise wedge dispute
processing.

## Summary

- The Vault holds every staked \$AURA token and tracks who stakes how
  much.
- Your tier and voting power are computed from your vaulted balance in
  real time.
- Wrong-side and silent dispute voters lose 0.1% of vaulted \$AURA per
  vote; that \$AURA funds rewards for correct voters.
- Only the bound Optimistic Oracle can invoke the Vault penalty operations,
  under the recorded round and funded-claim rules.
- Unlock requests use a seven-day wait and automatic free-portion delivery.
  Exact captured penalties and existing creator-capacity commitments retain
  their defined effect; governance and operational availability remain trust dependencies.
