## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Aura?">
    Aura is a decentralized prediction platform built on Alephium, where users
    can predict outcomes of future events, trade shares, and participate in
    community governance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What makes Aura different from other prediction markets?">
    Aura utilizes Alephium's scalable blockchain, offering lower fees, faster
    transactions, and a unique vaulting tier system that provides extensive
    user benefits.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Wallet & sign-in

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I create a wallet?">
    You don't install anything, because Aura has a built-in wallet. Choose
    **Sign up** and use your email or Google. Your account is created
    in one step with a generated username you can change in Settings, and you
    can add a passkey afterwards for one-tap sign-in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is my private key kept?">
    Inside a secure enclave, an isolated machine that runs one reviewed program
    and nothing else. Your key is created there and used there, and it is
    encrypted at rest so a server or database breach does not expose it. It
    never reaches your browser or your device. Aura holds no standing
    credential that can move your funds, because signing requires a short-lived
    permission issued to your own logged-in session. See
    [Your Aura Wallet](/getting-started/passkeys) for the full design.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see transaction pop-ups?">
    Once you're signed in, ordinary actions sign in the background, and
    clicking **Buy** or **Vote** is the approval. Sending funds to an address
    outside Aura is the exception, and it always asks you to confirm the
    destination and amount first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I lose my device or my passkey?">
    Sign in with your email or Google from any device and your balance is
    there. A passkey is a faster way in rather than the key to your account, so
    losing one never locks you out.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Deposits & withdrawals

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I deposit from Ethereum or another chain?">
    Click **Deposit**, pick your source chain (Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, or
    BNB Chain), and send USDC/USDT to your personal deposit address from any
    wallet or exchange. Your USD balance updates automatically once the
    deposit confirms.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long do deposits take?">
    Most deposits are credited within moments of confirming on the source
    chain. Large deposits take a routed path that settles in 30 to 90 minutes,
    and the deposit dialog shows which tier applies plus live status either
    way.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are there deposit fees?">
    Yes. Aura charges 0.30% on instant deposits and withdrawals. Large amounts
    settle on a routed path that charges 0.10% plus the actual network cost of
    moving the funds, which usually comes out lower overall. You also pay the
    standard network fee on the chain you send from. The dialog shows the exact
    fee before you send anything. See
    [Deposits & Withdrawals](/getting-started/bridging).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I buy in with a card?">
    Yes. The deposit dialog includes a card onramp that delivers stablecoins
    straight to your deposit address, where they are credited the same way as
    any other deposit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I withdraw to Ethereum or another chain?">
    Yes. Click **Withdraw**, pick the destination chain and address, and
    review the quote (which shows the exact amount that will arrive,
    network fee included) before confirming.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Token & vaulting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the $AURA token?">
    \$AURA is Aura's governance token. Vaulting (staking) \$AURA unlocks voting
    power, fee-sharing opportunities, and market creation privileges.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I stake (vault) $AURA tokens?">
    Visit the **Vault** section on the Aura website, input the number of
    tokens you wish to stake, and confirm the transaction via your connected
    wallet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I withdraw staked tokens?">
    Request an amount within the Vault's displayed unlock limit. Only one
    request may be outstanding. After seven days, Aura automatically delivers
    the free portion to your original wallet; captured penalty claims can
    retain part of it. Recent proposal uses and outgoing slot allocations can
    limit new requests. See [Staking & Vaulting \$AURA](/tokens/staking).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Market creation & trading

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I create a new market?">
    You need available creation capacity: personal capacity starts at **Scout**
    with 10,000 active AURA, or an accepted borrowed slot can supply capacity
    where lending is enabled. Open **Propose Market**, complete the form, and
    submit it for community voting. See [Create a market](/markets/creating-markets).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a limit on how many markets I can propose?">
    Personal capacity is one slot per whole 10,000 active AURA. Each counted
    proposal or linked-bundle root uses one slot for a rolling 24 hours;
    rejection, cancellation or deployment does not free it early. Borrowed
    slots follow their accepted quota and conditions, and linked bundles have
    separate tier limits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What fees are involved in trading?">
    A small, transparent **2.5%** trading fee is applied to each transaction
    conducted in USDT.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Market outcomes & disputes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is the outcome of a market determined?">
    When an event is decidable under its rules, a user can propose an outcome.
    It becomes final after the challenge window unless someone disputes it;
    disputed outcomes go through the commit/reveal vote.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I disagree with a proposed market outcome?">
    You can initiate a dispute by posting a dispute bond before the challenge
    window closes. The dispute then opens the commit/reveal voting process.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can vote on disputed outcomes?">
    You need at least **Iron tier** (60,000 vaulted \$AURA), completed Oracle
    activation after its 24-hour delay and keeper processing, and eligibility
    in that round. Votes are tier-weighted, and backing a losing outcome
    slashes 0.1% of your vaulted \$AURA. That cost is what keeps votes honest,
    so there is no separate check on whether you hold a position.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
