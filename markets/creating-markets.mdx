Aura puts market creation in the hands of the community. Stakers at **Scout** tier or above can propose new prediction markets on any topic, including sports,
politics, crypto, and real-world events, and earn a share of the trading fees
on every market they ship.

## Proposal requirements

Before submitting a proposal, you'll need:

- **Available creation capacity**. Personal capacity starts at Scout with 10,000
  active AURA. Where slot lending is enabled, an accepted borrowed slot can supply
  capacity without transferring the owner's stake or voting rights. See
  [Tier Structure](/tokens/tier-structure) and
  [Staking & Vaulting \$AURA](/tokens/staking).
- **A small ALPH deposit**, around 0.5 ALPH per market. This is refundable
  storage rent rather than a fee: Alephium charges it to hold your proposal's
  on-chain records, and it comes back as those records are freed. The portion
  reserved for the market contract itself is refunded if your proposal is
  **rejected**, or funds the new market's storage if it's **approved**. Aura
  converts the ALPH from your USD balance, so you never source it yourself.

## Market creation flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the proposal form">
    Click **Propose Market** at the top right of the Aura site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    - **Market type and question**. Choose a regular market, linked event, or
      sports market. State the event or outcome clearly; linked and sports pages
      group related **Options** under one event or game.
    - **Category**. Pick a top-level category so users can find your
      market.
    - **Resolution rules**. Describe exactly what conditions resolve the
      market YES, NO, or to each option.
    - **Sources & images**. List authoritative sources voters can use to
      verify the outcome, and add a relevant image to drive engagement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit + sign">
    Confirm the transaction in your wallet. The deposit is locked into a
    proposal contract.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Community voting">
    Your proposal enters a **24-hour community voting window**. Stakers
    review and vote on each proposal, weighted by their tier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approval">
    After voting closes, automation ranks the authenticated proposals, applies
    the existing volume-dependent acceptance percentage and daily cap, and
    checks duplicates. It freezes the selection before executing approvals
    and refunds. A missing required check or unfinished transaction delays
    progress; a successful submission alone does not prove deployment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Market limits

Personal capacity is the whole number of 10,000-AURA units in active stake.
Each counted proposal or linked-bundle root uses one slot for a rolling 24 hours.
Rejection, cancellation or deployment does not free that use early. A bundle
uses one capacity source; its components cannot combine different owners' slots.

An accepted outgoing grant reserves its quota against the slot owner's capacity.
Incoming slots do not change the recipient's tier or voting power. Supported grant
conditions can restrict categories, market types, bundle size, dates and usage;
reputation, Top Creator and external approval are not lending conditions.

  See [Binary Market Best Practices](/tutorials/binary-market-best-practices)

  for guidance on writing markets that gain support and pass curation.
</Tip>

## Linked markets

For events with multiple related outcomes such as election races, league seasons,
or playoff brackets, you can submit a **linked proposal** that bundles up to
**100 related markets**, subject to the capacity source's tier and accepted
terms, under one voting decision. The master counts in that inclusive limit. If the bundle is approved,
all of its child markets deploy together with shared metadata, so the UI can
group them into a single "event" page.

Large bundles are submitted across **several transactions** rather than one,
because Alephium caps how much script bytecode a single transaction can carry.
The form handles the batching for you and shows progress as it goes; you just
sign each step.

Linked proposals are ideal for:

- League winners + division winners + playoff appearances
- Multi-stage tournaments (group stage, knockouts, final)
- Election cycles with primaries, conventions, and the general

You'll find the linked-proposal mode in the same proposal form, under
**Linked / event-style markets**.
