## Balances and portfolio value

Your portfolio combines confirmed wallet collateral with the current quoted
value of open positions. Available-to-trade collateral accounts for reservations
and active wallet operations, so it can differ from confirmed ownership.

A missing balance, position or required quote makes the total unavailable. A
verified empty account can show zero. Open-position values are estimates, and
the chart does not reconstruct their historical market value. History rows and
expected payouts do not authorize spending.

## Account updates

Account events trigger fresh reads. A replay reset starts that read/subscription
sequence again. Switching wallets clears or masks the previous account's content;
late results from the previous wallet must not populate the new account.

The account view combines sources with their own authority and freshness. It is
not one atomic snapshot across the chain, trading engine, wallet and indexer.
Unavailable upstream evidence remains unavailable.

## History and CSV

History includes proposals, fills, Vault activity, resolution bonds, direct fee
rewards, market winnings and supported wallet transfers. Vault activity includes
deposits, activation, unlock requests, returned AURA and slashes. New Parlays are
unavailable and there is no Parlays History filter.

Every source page must complete before the view can export CSV. A source error
or repeated cursor disables export. Retained rows for the same wallet are marked stale
after an outage; they are not transferred to another wallet.

CSV follows your visible filters and sort order, including incoming/outgoing
signs and occurrence times. It retains displayed precision, so it is an activity
export, not an exact base-unit accounting ledger. A direct fee reward's allocation
day does not establish its payment time; an unfinalized allocation is not paid cash.

## Realized P/L

The P/L view recognizes profit and loss when positions are sold or finally
resolved. Buying shares adds their price and trading fee to cost basis; an open
buy contributes zero realized P/L. A sale subtracts the corresponding average
cost basis from proceeds after fees.

Final resolution values winning shares at one collateral token each and losing
shares at zero, then subtracts their remaining cost basis. A losing position
therefore records a loss without a payout. A winning payout may still be pending;
when it arrives, it is not counted as profit a second time.

For retained historical Parlays, open stakes contribute zero. A confirmed
terminal result records actual returns minus stake, including a confirmed zero
return. Finalized direct rewards are included as income. Wallet deposits,
withdrawals, Vault movements and changes in estimated open-position value are
excluded.

Amounts are accumulated exactly from decimal strings before the headline is
formatted to two places. Chart coordinates have display precision limits.
Invalid amounts, unsupported precision, missing cost-basis evidence or incomplete
sources make the complete total unavailable. Current wallet balances cannot
substitute for missing trading history.

A confirmed amount without an authoritative payment time may contribute to the
total while the chart remains unavailable. Missing payment time is not replaced
with an allocation date or the time a settlement was observed.

## Privacy and pending actions

Owner-private balances, positions, orders, settlements and watchlists require
your matching session. Other routes intentionally expose public activity; an
address in a URL does not itself make the route private.

The public pending-actions summary exposes governance and dispute action
metadata. Governance voting is actionable from the inclusive start until the
exclusive end. Reveal actions must match the current round and its open reveal
phase. The separate authenticated pending-reveals queue is owner-only, contains
private vote choices and omits salts.

A transaction ID alone does not prove successful delivery. Confirmation, script
execution, indexing and wallet recovery evidence describe different stages.
