<div className="crp-section">

A **prediction market** turns your take into a question with money on it. Your audience buys YES or NO shares, the price becomes the live odds (the crowd's real opinion, not the loudest reply) and whoever's right gets paid when the answer lands.

**The person who creates the question earns a cut of every dollar traded on it.** This prelaunch program targets 30% of the 2.5% trading fee through a gifted slot, with the slot owner retaining none of its creator allocation.

The current protocol's tier allocations range from 0.4% to 40% of trading fees; none is exactly 30%. The program's backing and accepted terms must be aligned before launch. Later reductions in the slot owner's tier can reduce dependent markets' rates. See [Creator rewards](/programs/creator-rewards) for current policy and automatic payout timing.
