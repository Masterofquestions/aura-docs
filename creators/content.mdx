## Film the moment you decided to make the market

Imagine a streamer announces a Friday subathon. Every subscription adds a minute to the countdown, and the stream ends when it hits zero. You find the last broadcast, turn to the camera, and say, **“Last time, chat kept this streamer live for 30 hours. Can they make it to 48 this weekend?”** Put that moment first. Even someone who missed the announcement knows what to watch for.

1. **Take them through the story.** Show the announcement, the timer rules, and the moment the clock hit zero last time. If Grok helped you find that clip, show the clip. Tell viewers what makes you think this weekend will go differently.
2. **Show yourself making the market.** Record your screen as you propose **“Will the subathon last 48 hours?”** Show how the official broadcast settles it. A Friday noon start means the same event has to reach noon on Sunday.
3. **Come back to the opening moment.** Once the market goes live, show the question and odds. Finish the thought on camera. **“Last time they made it to Saturday night. I think chat gets them to Sunday noon this time. I made a market on it.”**
4. **Give viewers one thing to do.** On Instagram, try **“Comment AURA and I'll DM you the market or show you how to make your own.”** Someone following the streamer can take a side. Someone curious about making a market can see how you did it. That same DM promotes your live market and earns you referral fees when new users sign up through your Aura referral link and trade.

Start on camera when you can. Try reading a fan’s prediction out loud, then cut to the moment in the last broadcast that makes you agree or disagree. Record the research first and choose the hook in the edit.

## Build a dedicated niche page (Canvas UGC)

A Canvas UGC page is a dedicated account where you or a creator you hire covers one niche through its markets. Pick a beat you could cover even on a day you have nothing to launch. For a streamer-drama page, that might mean covering surprise guests, creator splits, and subathons.

Warm up a new account by participating in that niche. Follow the people who break stories, reply when you have something to add, and post a few original takes. See which stories bring the same viewers back. When you launch a market, those viewers already know why they'd want your take. A general account can cover whatever breaks. If you cover both creator drama and state politics regularly, separate pages give each group a reason to follow.

## Turn “comment AURA” into a DM

Instagram is a good home for the original Reel because you can deliver the exact market to someone who asks. Connect an Instagram professional account to ManyChat, choose the Reel, and set **AURA** as the comment keyword. If you use an opening DM, give it a **Send me the links** button. After the tap, send the viewer these choices. [ManyChat setup guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17742037-quick-automation-auto-dm-links-from-comments)

| DM link | Where it goes |
| - | - |
| **Trade this market** | The live Aura market from the video. You earn your creator share when people trade on your market, however they found it. |
| **Create your own market** | The [creator Discord](https://discord.gg/NKYBQVSHeH) for feedback on an idea, fresh hooks, and the weekly clip calendar. |
| **New to Aura? Sign up here** | Your Aura signup referral link. You earn 10% of the trading fees paid by accounts that sign up through it, including on other markets. |

Test the DM from a second account. When you publish a Reel about a different market, point its automation to that market's URL.

## Cross-post the story and clip the best moments

Put the vertical cut on Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts. Change the last line to fit each platform's link options. On X or Threads, turn the same research into a short thread: the announcement, the previous 30-hour finish, your call, and the market link. Share the source and the question in a community or newsletter that already follows the story.

**Clipping** means pulling a moment out of a longer video, podcast, or livestream that makes sense on its own. Mark the timestamp when someone makes a prediction or challenges yours. Cut straight to that line, add captions, and show the market it's about. If you hire a clipper, send the footage you have permission to use, the timestamps, and the live link.

Prefer to stay off camera? Narrate a screen recording or turn the source, the question, and your take into a slideshow. AI can help assemble the slides. You can also hire a UGC creator to film in their own voice. Give them the source material and the question, then let them explain why they take their side. Pay a flat fee or offer a [share of creator rewards](/programs/creator-agreements).

## Put more behind the markets that take off

Try several markets and give each a first post. Watch which ones draw replies from people taking sides, clicks through to Aura, and actual trading volume. A clip with fewer views can deserve the second video if viewers keep coming back to trade. Use your [weekly review](/creators/measuring) to decide where your next hour or dollar goes.

When a market catches, make the follow-up people are asking for. A new statement, a last-second gift that keeps a stream going, a move in the odds, or the result itself gives you another story. Put a clipper, a paid UGC video, or an ad budget behind the one that's working.

## Paid ads: give people a story to track

Lead with the event and what people want to know next. In paid creator ads, don’t pitch Aura as a prediction market or tell viewers to “trade this market,” buy YES or NO, or sign up to trade. Give them a reason to follow the story on Aura.

> Last time, the clock hit zero after 30 hours. Can viewers keep this weekend’s subathon going for 48? **Track the live odds on Aura.**

Link directly to the forecast so viewers can follow the live odds. Anyone can view the odds on Aura. Trading is restricted by location. If you show a price in the ad, include the time you saw it. For examples of odds presented as news, see [Polymarket’s election coverage](https://polymarket.com/Elections) and [Kalshi’s election tracker example](https://news.kalshi.com/p/visit-electionodds-real-time-election-odds-tracker).
