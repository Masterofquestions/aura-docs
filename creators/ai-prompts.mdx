## Daily trend scan

Run this each morning, or schedule it in a tool that supports recurring searches. [Find the next breakout market](/creators/picking-markets)

```text
What are people fighting about online right now? Search public X posts,
replies, quote posts, and reporting from the past 24 hours. Scan politics,
culture, sports, tech, and business, or focus on [optional niche].
Find up to five stories, including one still spreading inside a niche.

For each story, tell me what set it off and link the original source.
Show me two posts that disagree and what suggests the fight is spreading.
Then go one or two moves past the headline. What could happen because
of this that people haven't started arguing about yet? Give me one
specific outcome worth investigating and explain why people would care.

Separate what you verified from what you inferred. If I paste yesterday's
brief [optional], repeat a story only if something changed.
```

## Market titles

```text
Here's one verified story: [what happened and source link].
Pitch three different outcomes people could trade on. What decision
could this trigger? What milestone could someone reach or miss?
Take at least one idea a step beyond what the coverage already says.

For each, write a short “Will ...?” title a fan could ask out loud.
Give it a deadline or a measurable finish line. In one sentence,
connect the original fact to that outcome. Keep the controversial
angle if the evidence supports it.
```

## Propose or pass

```text
Here are my candidate titles, deadlines, and sources: [paste].
For each, answer three questions:

1. Does this market already exist? Search Aura and competing platforms
   for the same outcome under different wording. Link close matches.
   Say which listings you couldn't check.
2. Who would put money on YES, who would buy NO, and what fact gives
   each side a reason to think they're right?
3. After Aura's 24-hour proposal vote, what development could bring
   traders back before the market closes? What source settles it?

Make one call: propose a specific title, share an existing Aura market,
or pass. Give the strongest reason for that call in two sentences.
```

Open the closest Aura match yourself before submitting.

## Resolution rules

```text
Market question: [title]
Finish line: [deadline and time zone, or event and duration]
Official source: [link]

Draft YES and NO rules that two people watching the same source could
apply. Spell out what counts and when we stop watching.

Test the likeliest close call for this particular market. For a 48-hour
subathon, what if the stream disconnects for two minutes and resumes
with the same countdown? For a law, what if it's signed before the
deadline but takes effect later? Choose a case that fits my question.
If the result depends on a choice I haven't made, ask me that question
before finalizing the wording.
```

## Launch posts

```text
Live market title and link: [paste]
Confirmed story and source: [paste]
My take and the moment in my research that convinced me: [paste]
Current YES odds and timestamp: [paste what I saw]

Write a short Instagram Reel script and an X post in my voice.
Open the Reel with the strongest moment. Cut back to the source, show
me making the market, then return to my take. End with “Comment AURA”
for the market or the creator guide. On X, link directly to the market.
Use only the odds I supplied.
```

## Follow-up posts

```text
Live market question and link: [paste]
What's changed since my last post: [source, clip, or timestamp]
What I think it means: [my take]
Odds before and after, if I have them: [prices and timestamps]

Write a 20-second Reel. Open on the new moment and tell me what to show
on screen. Give just enough context for someone seeing the story for
the first time. Why might this change someone's mind about YES or NO?
End with one way to follow the market. Mention a price move only
if I supplied both prices.
```

For the subathon, the opening line could be **“There were 12 seconds left. Then one viewer added 100 minutes.”** Show the clock jump before explaining the 48-hour market. [See the full example.](/creators/example)

## What should I promote next?

```text
Here's my post log: [market, post angle, time since posting, views,
clicks, tracked trading volume, creator and referral earnings, spend].
Markets still open and upcoming developments: [paste].

Compare posts over the same amount of time where possible. Which angle
brought people who actually traded? For each paid post, subtract spend
from the earnings I tracked for that promotion. If a number is missing,
say so.

Give me one next move: make an organic follow-up, pay to promote a
specific post, or move on. Name the market and angle, and use my
numbers to explain why in two sentences.
```

In the subathon example, the countdown Reel drove \$6,000 in trading but earned \$27 against a \$40 boost on day one. That makes the next move **another countdown clip, posted organically**. [See the post log.](/creators/measuring)
