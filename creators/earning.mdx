title: "How creators earn"
description: "Earn up to 40% of the trading fees on markets you create."

Every time a trade goes through on your market, Aura charges a 2.5% fee on each side. Your tier sets how much of those fees you earn. You earn that share whether the traders found the market through one of your posts or came across it on Aura.

## How trading volume drives your earnings

More trading volume generates more fees. At the same tier and split, a market that generates twice the fees earns you twice as much. Aura pays [creator rewards](/programs/creator-rewards) daily in USDT, even while the market is open.

Say your market asks whether a rookie will hit 25 home runs this season. When he hits two in one game, show what that does to his pace, post the latest odds, and link the market. Fans on either side have a reason to come back and trade. You earn a share of the fees either way.

## Where to start

Already have an audience? Start with the question they keep arguing about. Starting from zero? Pick a niche you follow and build a page around the questions people there already debate.

- **Create a market in your niche.** Stake at least 10,000 AURA (Scout) or accept a borrowed slot through [Creator agreements](/programs/creator-agreements). A borrowed slot uses the owner's tier.
- **Plan your first two posts.** Lead with the question and your take. Pick the next game, announcement, or vote that could change someone's mind, and follow up when it happens.

## Other ways to earn on Aura

| What you do | How you earn |
| - | - |
| [Refer a trader](/programs/referral-program) | Earn 10% of the trading fees someone pays after signing up through your referral link. That includes trades on other creators' markets. |
| [Provide liquidity](/programs/liquidity-rewards) | Keep qualifying limit orders near the midpoint to earn a share of the daily USDT pool. Traders can fill those orders, so size them for a position you're willing to hold. |
| [Work with another creator](/programs/creator-agreements) | Help promote their market for an agreed share of creator rewards, or lend a proposal slot and retain a share. Accept the terms in Creator Agreements. |
| [Propose or challenge a result](/markets/resolution-and-disputes) | Post a USDT bond to propose the outcome or challenge an incorrect one. An uncontested proposal returns your bond. In a dispute, the winner gets their bond back plus half the loser's bond. |
| [Vote on a disputed outcome](/programs/dispute-rewards) | Activated, eligible stakers at Iron tier or higher can vote. Winning voters share the \$AURA penalty pool and, on qualifying final YES/NO disputes, 10% of the market's trading fees. A losing vote costs stake. |

## Mistakes to avoid

<Warning>
The slot owner's tier sets the starting rate when the proposal is counted. Dropping a tier permanently lowers the rate on markets that depend on it. A later stake increase can help your next proposal. See [Tier structure](/tokens/tier-structure).
</Warning>

- **Chasing signups.** One active trader is worth more than fifty signups who never place an order.
- **Self-referral or wash trading.** You pay real trading fees when you trade between your own wallets. You can't refer yourself.
