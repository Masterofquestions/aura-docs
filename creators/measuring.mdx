Volume is the number that pays. A post with 100,000 views and no trades is worth less than a Discord message that moved \$5,000.

## What to track

| Metric | Where | What it tells you |
| - | - | - |
| Views, AURA comments, and link clicks | Platform analytics, ManyChat, link tracker | Which posts get attention and what viewers do next |
| Referral signups and their trading | **Refer & Earn** page | Whether attention turns into traders |
| Volume per market | **Dashboard**, Markets tab | Which markets your audience trades |
| Creator, referral, and liquidity payouts | **Dashboard**, **Refer & Earn**, and **Liquidity rewards** pages | What it adds up to |
| Proposal approvals | **Dashboard**, Proposals tab | Whether your proposals pass curation |

## Find where people drop off

Say a Reel gets plenty of **AURA** comments. Open the [ManyChat flow](/creators/content) and see which button people tap. If few make it to the links, test the opening DM from a second account. Then follow the path they chose.

| What they chose | What to check |
| - | - |
| **Trade this market** | If people click but few trade, open the market on your phone. Does it match the question in the Reel? Are there orders someone can actually fill? |
| **Create your own market** | Check Discord invite joins. If the behind-the-scenes clip brings in aspiring creators, film yourself making the next proposal. |
| **New to Aura? Sign up here** | Check referred signups, then see who makes a first trade and comes back. If signups stall, send the [getting-started guide](/getting-started/connecting). |

## Keep a simple log

Give each post a row. When you pay for ads, UGC, or clipping, record the spend alongside the creator and referral fees you can trace to the promotion. Here’s an example comparing the first day of two Reels about a weekend subathon. Viewers add time to a livestream by subscribing. The market asks whether they can keep it going for 48 hours.

| Reel | Views | Market clicks | Tracked trading volume | Spend | Earnings |
| - | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: |
| Streamer announces a weekend subathon | 40,000 | 80 | \$1,400 | \$0 | \$6 |
| The clock is running. Can viewers keep it going for 48 hours? | 12,000 | 240 | \$6,000 | \$40 | \$27 |

The clip showing the countdown got fewer views but brought in more trading. When a gift saves the stream with seconds to spare, that’s your next Reel. The \$40 promotion earned \$27 in fees in its first day, so make the follow-up organically before buying another boost.

## Weekly review

Set aside 15 minutes:

1. Which market had the most volume? What's the next development people could trade on?
2. Which post drove the most trades? Which brought in new traders or Discord joins?
3. Where you spent money, did the **additional creator and referral fees** cover the cost? What could you repeat without paying?

Pick one market for your next video and one to stop promoting. Put more money behind a post when the earnings justify it. Once a market resolves, take the format that worked into your next launch.
