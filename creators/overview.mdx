Create a market and earn up to **40% of its trading fees**. Your tier sets your share. This playbook shows you how to pick a question, launch the market, and get people trading.

New to prediction markets? Start with [How markets work](/markets/overview). Trade a few markets before you create one. You'll write better questions once you've watched how odds move.
