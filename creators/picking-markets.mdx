description: "Start with breaking news and find the question people will want to trade once someone asks it."
---

When a story breaks, everyone gets the same headline. Your edge is figuring out what just became possible. Who gained power? What can they do with it? Who could stop them? Follow those questions far enough and you can open a market while everyone else is still recapping the news.

A question that would make a sponsor uncomfortable can be exactly the one worth asking on Aura. If you look on Polymarket and no one has listed it, you can bring it straight to Aura's proposal vote. Think of the question someone would vote to see go live, send to a friend who disagrees, and come back to trade when the story moves.

## Go two moves past the headline

Imagine it's midterm night. Against the odds, Texas has elected a Democratic governor. The networks are talking about turnout and the upset. You're thinking about weed.

Why? Suppose the incoming governor has publicly backed legalization. A bill that once faced a veto might now get a signature. It still has to get through the legislature. Suddenly there's a real case for YES, a real case for NO, and a question that could travel well beyond Texas:

**Will Texas legalize recreational marijuana in 2027?**

You got there from the election result. The market could be the first thing that gets people talking about legalization that night. For the rules, define what counts: Texas must enact a law allowing adults 21 and over to possess marijuana for recreational use by 11:59 p.m. Central on December 31, 2027. Check the governor-elect's actual position before proposing, and name the record that settles the market. In this example, that's [Texas Legislature Online](https://capitol.texas.gov/).

## The argument test

Picture a producer at Fox News or CNN seeing your market tomorrow. **Would the question itself give them a segment?** “Traders are putting odds on Texas legalizing weed after last night's upset” gives them something new to cover. A controversial question can start the argument. You can be first to ask it if you can show why the outcome is now possible.

Keep the controversy in the subject and the wording clean. Ask the hard question directly. Leave out calls for violence, obvious spam, derogatory language, and profanity.

- **Make the strongest case on each side.** People can have opposite opinions about legalization and still agree the bill will fail. Find the facts that could make someone back YES with money.
- **Check who got there first.** Search Aura and competing platforms using the people, event, and outcome in your question. If Aura already has that market, share it. If everyone covers the headline, look for a decision they've missed.
- **Leave room for the odds to move.** Allow for Aura's 24-hour proposal vote. Identify one development people could trade on before the deadline and the public source that settles it.

## Where to pick up a story

On **X**, follow beat reporters, local organizers, and people inside the niche. Open the original post and read the replies for names, dates, and leads. On **Reddit**, find the community that knows the history. Use **Google** to check the interview, bill, filing, or announcement for yourself. Google Trends can help you see whether searches for the headline are rising. Your market question may be too new to show up there.

An existing audience helps you recognize a surprise early. A *Love Island* fan might know exactly why one contestant's departure puts the reunion in doubt. Starting from zero? Follow a story you can't stop reading and learn who makes the next decision. If you cover several unrelated niches regularly, each can have its own account.

## AI prompts for market research

Paste the first prompt into [Grok on X](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/about-grok) for a shortlist of markets. Run it as written for a broad scan, or add a niche you want to cover.

### What's hot today, and what's the market?

```text
What's blowing up today? Search the last 24 hours of public X posts,
replies, and quote posts. Pick three stories people are fighting about,
including one that's taking off inside a niche. For each:

1. What happened? What set off the fight today? Link the original source
   and two posts that show why people disagree.
2. What could happen next because of this? Name who makes the call.
   Look for a consequence people haven't started debating yet.
3. Pitch one “Will ___ by ___?” market. Give a credible reason for
   YES and for NO, and name the public record that would settle it.

Which one would you propose on Aura today? Tell me why people would
care enough to trade it. Make clear what's confirmed and what's your inference.
```

### Go deeper on the best story

```text
Take [story from the shortlist]. Suppose the obvious next move happens.
Who has to respond, and what choice do they face? Give me two market
titles about different consequences. Put a deadline on each.
Which one's odds could become a headline in their own right, and why?
```

Open the original source and search Aura for the question before you propose. If you've got several contenders, use the [AI prompt library](/creators/ai-prompts#propose-or-pass) to compare them and draft rules for the one you pick.
