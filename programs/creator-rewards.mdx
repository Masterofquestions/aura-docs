Create a market that people want to trade and you earn a slice of every trade on
it, for the entire life of the market. The slot owner's tier history sets the
creator allocation, including when you use a borrowed slot. Accepted sharing
terms determine how that allocation is distributed.

## What you earn

Creator allocations start between **0.4% and 40% of the 2.5% trading fee**, before accepted sharing. The source policy uses whole fee points, not voting weights:

| Tier | AURA required | Proposal votes | Creator fee points | Share of the 250-point trading fee |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Degenerate | 1,000,000 | 30 | 100 | 40% |
| Diamond | 833,333 | 22 | 83 | 33.2% |
| Platinum | 666,667 | 16 | 67 | 26.8% |
| Gold | 500,000 | 11 | 50 | 20% |
| Silver | 333,333 | 7 | 33 | 13.2% |
| Bronze | 166,667 | 3 | 17 | 6.8% |
| Iron | 60,000 | 1 | 6 | 2.4% |
| Builder | 25,000 | 0 | 3 | 1.2% |
| Scout | 10,000 | 0 | 1 | 0.4% |

Amounts are calculated in token base units with integer rounding. The creator allocation is carved from the existing fee and does not add another trader charge.

<Note>
The slot owner's tier history sets the ceiling at counted proposal use. Dropping below a tier threshold permanently lowers the rate for dependent markets; restaking cannot raise that older rate. A borrowed slot uses its owner's tier history, not the recipient's stake.
</Note>

## How it gets paid

Current source schedules creator fees and accepted sharing allocations for automatic daily payouts, including while a market remains unresolved. Processing uses daily UTC windows and catches up after missed runs.

Fees first move from the Clearinghouse into the recorded reward escrow. Workers calculate each accepted allocation and deliver it to the recorded wallet. While an earning period is open, a small rounding remainder can stay pending; the exact remainder is delivered after that period closes and its calculations finish. Without sharing, this rounding holdback is zero.

Where enabled, borrowed-slot terms retain the accepted slot-owner share, and additional reward commitments require the recipient's acceptance before their future interval begins. Accepted commitments cannot be removed, reduced or reassigned by the creator. They never change the public market creator.

There is no user claim button, claim fee or email-code step. Your dashboard distinguishes earned, awaiting payout and actually paid amounts, with payment history. Service availability, confirmed chain observations and schedule maintenance affect timing. A transfer into reward escrow is shown as pending until the recipient wallet payment is confirmed.

## Making this worth doing

Your earnings are a percentage of volume, so the whole game is attracting volume.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stake before you propose, not after">
    The initial ceiling comes from the slot owner's tier. Iron starts at six
    creator points out of 250 fee points (2.4% of the fee). Staking to Degenerate
    later cannot raise that older ceiling, and a downward tier crossing can lower it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write rules that cannot be argued with">
    Ambiguous resolution criteria are the main reason markets end up disputed,
    and a qualifying dispute allocates 10% of gross fees to voters. This reserve
    does not reduce your recorded creator allocation. Name the exact source and the exact condition. See
    [Creating markets](/markets/creating-markets).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick questions with a natural audience">
    Volume follows interest. A market about something nobody is arguing about
    will not trade, no matter how cleanly it is written.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it enough time to trade">
    Give a market time to attract volume. A clear question with an interested
    audience can keep earning while it remains open and tradable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seed your own liquidity">
    A market with an empty book does not attract traders. Quoting it yourself
    also earns [liquidity rewards](/programs/liquidity-rewards), so the two
    programs compound.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Costs and limits

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Proposals per wallet | 25 open at a time |
| ALPH deposit | Refundable storage rent, returned when the market is torn down |
| Minimum tier to propose | See [Tier structure](/tokens/tier-structure) |
| Acceptance | Proposals go to a governance vote before becoming markets |

Proposing is not the same as creating. Your proposal has to pass a
[governance vote](/governance/proposal-voting) before it becomes a market, and
only accepted proposals earn anything.
