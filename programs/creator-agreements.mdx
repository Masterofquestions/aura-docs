Creator Agreements is the private workspace for proposal-slot offers and market
reward-sharing agreements. You can find it from the app when you have an active
creator tier, an agreement, or an offer waiting for your decision.

## One place for offers and agreements

The workspace keeps the two agreement types together:

- **Proposal slots** let a slot owner offer proposal capacity to another Aura
  account. The slot owner's staked AURA, tier, voting weight, and Oracle
  exposure stay with the owner; the recipient receives only the capacity
  described by the accepted terms.
- **Reward sharing** lets a market creator offer a recorded share of eligible
  creator earnings to another account. The recipient must accept before the
  future sharing interval begins.

These are agreements between accounts, not transfers of staked AURA. Accepted
terms are recorded against the specific agreement and market or event they
cover.

## Find what needs attention

The page groups agreements by the overall market context. A linked event or a
sports game can show one grouped offer for its related options instead of making
you review every row in isolation.

Use the status tabs to switch between:

- **Needs review** — offers waiting for your decision.
- **Current** — active accepted agreements.
- **History** — ended, rejected, cancelled, or otherwise completed records.

The search field finds an agreement by its market, event, or account details.
The direction filter separates agreements you received from agreements you sent.
When several options share the same terms, the group can be accepted or managed
as one bundle; open the group when you need to inspect individual options.

## Create an offer

Choose **Create** in the page header. The form switches between **Proposal
slots** and **Reward sharing** without taking you to a separate page.

For a proposal-slot offer, choose the recipient, slot quota, terms, and the
share of creator rewards that the slot owner will retain. For reward sharing,
choose the recipient, the markets or linked/sports bundle, the sharing rate, and
the active interval. Review the exact recipients, markets, and terms before you
sign.

The recipient gets the offer in Creator Agreements and can accept or decline it.
An offer is not active merely because it was created; the recorded agreement
and its status are the source of truth.

## How payouts work

Creator and accepted reward-sharing earnings use the daily UTC payout schedule
described in [Creator rewards](/programs/creator-rewards). The workspace shows
the agreement and its status; the dashboard history shows earned, pending, and
confirmed paid amounts. There is no claim button and no email-code step for
ordinary agreement actions.
