Aura pays out to the people who make the market work. There are four programs,
each aimed at a different job, and they stack: nothing stops you from creating
markets, quoting on them, referring traders, and voting on disputes.

Aura is pre-mainnet. The schedules below describe current source behavior;
daily creator payouts remain subject to release verification.

The USDT programs draw from the **2.5% trading fee** on executed volume.
Dispute voters may also receive AURA from the recorded penalty pool; that is
a separate asset and source, not another USDT fee or newly minted tokens.

## Where the fee goes

Every fill pays 2.5%. That fee funds the USDT allocations below; the AURA
penalty pool is separate:

| Slice | Goes to |
| --- | --- |
| Initially 0.4% to 40% | Creator allocation under the slot owner's tier history, before accepted sharing |
| 10% | The daily liquidity rewards pool |
| 10% | The referrer of the trader, if they have one |
| 10% provisionally reserved | Winning voters if the final dispute qualifies; otherwise released to treasury |
| Remainder | Protocol treasury, which funds development and the programs above |

Because the creator's slice scales with tier, the treasury's share is what
flexes. A market created by a Degenerate-tier staker gives more to the creator
and less to the treasury.

## How payouts reach you

Each program has its own payout schedule and eligibility checks.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="After settlement" icon="flag-checkered">
    Referral and dispute payments follow their recorded settlement paths.
    Aura sends eligible payouts automatically; an escrow handoff or pending
    history entry is not a confirmed wallet payment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Daily" icon="calendar-day">
    Creator and accepted sharing payouts use daily UTC windows, including before
    resolution, with any final rounding remainder paid after its earning period
    closes. Liquidity rewards are scored per day for next-day delivery. Both
    depend on completed calculations, confirmed evidence and working services.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
A `TOO_EARLY` dispute round pays no voter reward: its AURA pool carries into a
later round and the market returns to trading. Creator reward epochs retain
their separate checkpoint and distribution rules. See
[Resolution and disputes](/markets/resolution-and-disputes).
</Note>

## Which program fits you

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I have good ideas for markets" icon="lightbulb">
    [Creator rewards](/programs/creator-rewards). Your earnings scale with how
    much volume your market attracts, so this rewards judgment about what people
    want to trade. Your available slot and its owner's tier history set the
    initial allocation; accepted sharing and later tier reductions also apply.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I have capital and want yield" icon="layer-group">
    [Liquidity rewards](/programs/liquidity-rewards). Quote both sides near the
    midpoint. Paid daily regardless of resolution, so the cycle is short.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I have an audience" icon="user-plus">
    [Referral program](/programs/referral-program). One link, and you earn on
    everything your referrals trade for as long as they trade.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I follow a subject closely" icon="scale-balanced">
    [Dispute voting](/programs/dispute-rewards). Correct votes can earn AURA and
    qualifying USDT rewards. Backing a losing outcome costs you staked \$AURA.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
